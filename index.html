I am a postdoctoral researcher at the Hybrid Manufacturing Innovation Engineering Center and the Intelligent Additive Manufacturing Lab, School of Mechanical Engineering, Pusan National University. My research integrates advanced experimental characterization, computational modeling, and process monitoring to understand, predict, and optimize process–structure–property relationships across laser powder bed fusion (L-PBF), powder-fed directed energy deposition (DED), and wire arc additive manufacturing (WAAM).

01 · Biography

Professional biography

I am a mechanical engineer and researcher working at the intersection of metal additive manufacturing, materials engineering, and artificial intelligence. My work improves the quality, repeatability, and performance of additively manufactured metallic components through process optimization, materials characterization, in-process monitoring, and predictive modeling.

My research spans laser powder bed fusion, powder-fed directed energy deposition, wire arc additive manufacturing, and laser cladding. Current work includes Inconel 625–TiC and TaC/Ti-6Al-4V composite deposition, acoustic-monitoring-assisted reuse of Alloy 939 powder, TPMS lattice design, and lightweighting of industrial components through design for additive manufacturing. I am particularly interested in physics-aware machine learning, defect prediction, and circular, sustainable metal AM.

01 · Vision

Research vision

My research aims to create reliable, interpretable, and industry-relevant approaches for metal additive manufacturing by combining experiments, numerical and thermodynamic modeling, sensor-driven monitoring, and machine learning. The long-term goal is to support robust process windows, defect-aware manufacturing, sustainable repair and feedstock reuse, and qualification-ready metallic AM components.

Track A

L-PBF quality & design

Porosity, hardness, surface roughness, scanning strategy, hatch spacing, build orientation, lattice and TPMS architectures, and repeatability.

Track B

DED, WAAM & cladding

Composite and functionally graded coatings, repair, dilution and interface control, wear and corrosion resistance, and powder reuse.

Track C

AI for manufacturing

Machine learning, physics-aware and hybrid modeling, acoustic and multimodal monitoring, feature importance, and process correction.
